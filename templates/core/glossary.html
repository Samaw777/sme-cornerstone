Evidence and financial terms

The same labels appear everywhere: in the app, the passport, the PDF and the partner API.

{% evidence_badge "system_verified" %}

Imported from an authorised external source (for example a connected bank account), with the source's own identifier and the original event preserved. Demo connectors are always labelled Demo and are not real verification.

{% evidence_badge "evidence_supported" %}

Entered or imported by the business and backed by a linked receipt, invoice or statement. Each document shows its validation status: file checks only, reviewed by platform staff, or rejected. Rejected documents don't count.

{% evidence_badge "self_reported" %}

Entered or imported by the business without independent verification. CSV uploads are self-reported. Useful context, never presented as verified.

{% evidence_badge "derived" %}

Calculated by the platform from identified records using a documented method (trend, variability, totals). Derived metrics are never counted as extra evidence.

Revenue
Money in from sales, services and other operating income. Loans and owner capital are not revenue.
Operating expenses
Money out for running the business. Loan repayments, owner drawings and equipment purchases are not operating expenses.
Net cash movement
All money in minus all money out for a month. It is not profit.
Accounting profit
Not calculated. It would need accruals, depreciation, inventory changes and tax, which this platform doesn't record.
Unknown
A month with no records is shown as unknown, never as zero.
Evidence coverage
Share of the gross value of counted records (money in + money out) in each evidence class. The denominator is always stated.
Data-quality indicator
A neutral prompt to check something (a gap, an unusual change, a possible duplicate). It is not a finding of error, fraud or creditworthiness.
Correction
An amendment or reversal with an author, time and reason. The original record is always kept.
Consent
Your explicit, time-limited, revocable permission for one institution to see named data for a named period and purpose.

The Business Financial Passport provides financial evidence and is not a credit decision.
